`Accept` weights are now read with the RFC 9110 §12.4.2 `qvalue` grammar (`0` to `1`, at most three decimals) instead of `Number()`. A `q` outside it — `q=`, `q=0x0`, `q=-1`, `q=1e-9`, `q=2`, `q=1.0001`, a quoted `q="0.5"` — is now malformed, handled the way `q=abc` already was: `Negotiation` reads it as the default weight `1` (so `q=` is no longer an explicit `q=0` refusal, and `q=2` no longer outranks every other range), and `Middleware.acceptIsAcceptable` / `Middleware.negotiate` skip the entry as they do any unparseable weight. So does the `ContentNegotiation` middleware every `HttpApi` endpoint runs (`Middleware.layer`, `Middleware.layerWith`, `Middleware.contentNegotiationLayer`, `Middleware.ContentNegotiationLive`): a lone `application/vnd.api+json;q=2` or `;q=1e-9` now answers 406 there, where it was accepted before; `Middleware.layerHostNegotiated` does not negotiate and is unaffected. Well-formed weights, including `q=0`, `q=0.000` and `Q=0`, are unchanged.
